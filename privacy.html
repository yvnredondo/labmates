LabMates Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

LabMates is a shared duty roster for a small group of medical technologists who work together. It is run by Yvan Redondo for coworkers, not as a commercial service.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, these come from your Google account. LabMates never sees your Google password.
  • Your roster: the duties, leave days, notes and settings you enter.

LabMates does not use ads, analytics or tracking, and does not collect patient information. Please don't enter any.

How it's used

  • Your name and roster are shown to other approved members of your group so you can see who is on duty.
  • Your email is visible only to the group's admin, so they can confirm who is asking to join.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in provider. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. A copy of your own roster is kept on your phone so the app works offline; it's removed when you sign out.

Deleting your data

You can clear your duties at any time under More → Clear all my duties. To delete your account completely, ask the group's admin, who can remove it and everything linked to it.

Questions

Contact the LabMates admin in your group.
